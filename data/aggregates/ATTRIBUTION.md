These derived CSVs are excluded from the repository's MIT software licence. Retain
this notice and the source terms when sharing them. No source provider endorses
the project or its interpretations. Full details: [SOURCE_TERMS.md](../SOURCE_TERMS.md).

- **wgm.csv:** Gallup (2019), *Wellcome Global Monitor – First Wave Findings*,
  copyright Wellcome; [2018 dataset and report](https://wellcome.org/insights/reports/wellcome-global-monitor-2018).
  [Report copyright notice, PDF p.2](https://cms.wellcome.org/sites/default/files/wellcome-global-monitor-2018.pdf#page=2):
  CC BY for data/text, version unspecified. [Wellcome's website default](https://wellcome.org/about-us/our-people/governance/privacy-and-terms)
  is [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Project changes:
  seven weighted proportions for selected adults and demographic groups. No photos.
  Collection support: Wellcome Trust and Gallup Organization Ltd, *Wellcome Global
  Monitor, 2018*, 2nd edition (2019), UK Data Service,
  [doi:10.5255/UKDA-SN-8466-2](https://doi.org/10.5255/UKDA-SN-8466-2);
  [catalog CC BY 4.0 notice](https://datacatalogue.ukdataservice.ac.uk/studies/study/8466).
  The actual workbook is separately pinned by SHA256 and the Wellcome notice.
- **palms.csv:** Andrew Kerr, David Lam and Martin Wittenberg, PALMS 1993–2025,
  version 4; DataFirst, Cape Town, producer/distributor, 2025;
  [doi:10.25828/gtr1-8r20](https://doi.org/10.25828/gtr1-8r20).
  [Dataset access policy](https://www.datafirst.uct.ac.za/dataportal/index.php/catalog/434):
  CC BY, version unspecified. Project changes: wave 92, ages 15–64, three weighted
  job-search-duration proportions. Underlying surveys include Statistics South Africa
  and SALDRU sources; source microdata are not distributed here.
- **pases.csv:** Andrew Kerr, Amy Thornton and Martin Wittenberg, PASES 1995–2024,
  version 1.1; DataFirst, Cape Town, producer/distributor, 2026;
  [doi:10.25828/49GK-P872](https://doi.org/10.25828/49GK-P872).
  [Dataset access policy](https://www.datafirst.uct.ac.za/dataportal/index.php/catalog/1083):
  CC BY, version unspecified. Project changes: 2024, ages 15–64, three weighted labour
  proportions derived from Statistics South Africa survey series. No source microdata.
- **hno.csv:** OCHA on behalf of the Humanitarian Country Team and partners,
  [Syria Humanitarian Needs](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/syrian-arab-republic-humanitarian-needs),
  HNO 2024 PiN/severity workbook, 2024-04-01 revision. The
  [official API](https://data.humdata.org/api/3/action/package_show?id=syrian-arab-republic-humanitarian-needs)
  gives CC BY, version unspecified; [published licence link](http://www.opendefinition.org/licenses/cc-by).
  Project changes: three unweighted governorate means of valid subdistrict severity codes.
- **ged.csv:** Uppsala Conflict Data Program, GED Global version 26.1;
  [data, terms and citations](https://ucdp.uu.se/downloads/),
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Cite Shawn Davies,
  Therése Pettersson and Magnus Öberg (2026), *Organized violence 1989–2025,
  and violent political protests*, Journal of Peace Research,
  [doi:10.1093/jopres/xjag046](https://doi.org/10.1093/jopres/xjag046), and Ralph
  Sundberg and Erik Melander (2013), *Introducing the UCDP Georeferenced Event
  Dataset*, Journal of Peace Research 50(4). UCDP is part of DEMSCORE, supported by
  Swedish Research Council grant 2021-00162. Project changes: Syrian governorate
  event counts in 2024 and January–April 2025, equal parsed event dates, `log1p`.

Afrobarometer data and derivatives are absent. A locally combined complete v4
package has additional redistribution obligations and is not cleared by this notice.
